Turn Over Ceremony

Council of Engineering and Technology Students are now giving the responsibility to the new officers (2026-2027)

August 5 2026

The Turn Over Ceremony marks a meaningful transition in the CETS Organization as the outgoing officers formally pass their responsibilities, duties, and commitments to the newly elected officers. It symbolizes the continuity of leadership and the trust placed in the new set of officers to guide the organization toward its goals and aspirations.

Through this ceremony, the outgoing officers share their experiences and lessons, while the new officers accept the responsibility of serving the organization and its members with dedication, integrity, and commitment. This event serves as a reminder that leadership is not only about holding a position, but also about making a positive impact and leaving a lasting legacy for the officers who will follow.

The newly elected officers of the Council of Engineering and Technology Students (CETS) at USTP Claveria are ready to begin the school year with fresh perspectives, innovative ideas, and a shared commitment to serving the student body. With their diverse talents, leadership abilities, and individual goals, they aim to introduce meaningful initiatives that promote academic excellence, strengthen student engagement, and foster unity among students of the College of Engineering and Technology. Through creative programs, collaborative activities, improved communication, and student-centered projects, the new set of officers aspires to create more opportunities for learning, leadership development, and personal growth. They also seek to embrace innovation, listen to students' concerns, and transform their ideas into practical solutions that contribute to a more inclusive, productive, and supportive campus environment. Above all, the new CETS officers are committed to making this school year more meaningful and memorable by turning their shared vision into tangible accomplishments and building a stronger, more united engineering and technology community.
