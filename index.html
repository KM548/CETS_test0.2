About CETs

The College of Engineering and Technology (CETs) of the University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines (USTP) – Claveria Campus is committed to producing competent, innovative, and globally competitive professionals in the fields of engineering and information technology. The college provides quality education through industry-relevant curricula, hands-on laboratory experiences, research, extension services, and community engagement. CETs aims to develop graduates who possess technical expertise, critical thinking, leadership, and ethical values.

Through modern learning facilities, dedicated faculty members, and partnerships with industry and government institutions, students are equipped with the knowledge and practical skills needed to address real-world challenges and contribute to national development. Beyond academics, the college encourages students to participate in organizations, seminars, workshops, competitions, and outreach programs that foster personal growth, teamwork, and social responsibility. These opportunities help students become well-rounded professionals who are prepared to lead and innovate in their chosen careers.

As one of the academic colleges of USTP Claveria, the College of Engineering and Technology continues to uphold the university's commitment to excellence in science, technology, engineering, and innovation while serving the needs of the community and the nation.

Announcements and Updates

THE CETS TURNOVER CEREMONY

September 19 2026


The CETs Turnover Ceremony marks a significant milestone in the College of Engineering and Technology Organization of USTP Claveria, symbolizing the official transition of leadership from the outgoing officers to the newly elected officers. This event celebrates the accomplishments of the previous administration while welcoming a new set of student leaders who are entrusted with the responsibility of serving the college and its students. During the ceremony, the newly elected officers formally accepted their roles and responsibilities through the turnover of organizational documents, symbols of leadership, and the administration of the Oath of Office.

By taking their oath, the officers pledged to uphold the values of integrity, accountability, excellence, and service, committing themselves to lead with dedication and to work for the welfare and development of the CETs community. The turnover ceremony also serves as a reminder that leadership is not merely a position but a commitment to inspire, represent, and support every student under the College of Engineering and Technology. As they begin their term, the new officers vow to promote unity, organize meaningful programs and activities, address students' concerns, and contribute to creating a positive and inclusive academic environment.

Through this tradition, the CETs Organization reaffirms its mission of developing responsible student leaders who are prepared to serve with honor, passion, and excellence while advancing the interests of the College of Engineering and Technology and its students.
