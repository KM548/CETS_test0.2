Your voice matters! The Council of Engineering and Technology Students (CETS) warmly invites all students who attended the General Assembly to share their thoughts, opinions, and experiences through the CETS General Assembly Feedback Form. This initiative aims to gather valuable insights regarding the event's presentations, program flow, activities, and overall experience. Whether you have positive feedback, suggestions, or areas you believe need improvement, your honest opinions are highly appreciated. All responses will remain anonymous, allowing everyone to express their thoughts freely and confidently. Through your feedback, we aim to improve future events, strengthen student engagement, and create more meaningful and enjoyable experiences for the entire CETS community.
