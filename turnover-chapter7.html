PART I — Chapter 7: Constraints, Views and SQL

The Turn Over Ceremony marks a meaningful transition in the CETS Organization as the outgoing officers formally pass their responsibilities, duties, and commitments to the newly elected officers. It symbolizes the continuity of leadership and the trust placed in the new set of officers to guide the organization toward its goals and aspirations. Through this ceremony, the outgoing officers share their experiences and lessons, while the new officers accept the responsibility of serving the organization and its members with dedication, integrity, and commitment. This event serves as a reminder that leadership is not only about holding a position, but also about making a positive impact and leaving a lasting legacy for the officers who will follow.
